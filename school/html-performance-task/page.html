Technology: The Good, The Bad, The Ugly

calendar_month December 30, 2020 · By Brandon Swenson

A balanced look at technology’s benefits, drawbacks, and risks, with practical ways to use digital tools more mindfully.

The Good

Technology helps people stay connected, work more efficiently, and reach information and education from almost anywhere. Digital tools can make everyday tasks easier and open opportunities that once depended on location.

The Bad

Constant access can make it harder to rest and disconnect. Long stretches on devices can also contribute to sedentary habits, physical strain, and stress.

The Ugly

Online anonymity and rapid sharing can amplify bullying and misinformation. The article encourages readers to be thoughtful about what they consume and how they interact online.

Finding Balance

Set boundaries that fit your life: silence nonessential notifications, take breaks from screens, and make some moments device-free. Regularly consider whether a tool is helping you reach your goals or demanding more time and attention than it gives back.
